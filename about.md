<h1>About</h1>

Hi, I’m Luke. I’ve been developing software commercially for over 10 years, mostly self-taught in my spare time while also learning on the job. My core expertise is in iOS development with Swift and SwiftUI, but I also enjoy exploring adjacent technologies when a project sparks my interest. A handful of my personal projects have even made it into production under my own Apple Developer account.  

I first became curious about programming in my early teens, tinkering with Visual Basic, and later picked it up seriously in my early twenties. Back in 2012, I began teaching myself Objective-C from YouTube videos on a 2009 MacBook Pro, and I’ve been hooked on building apps ever since.  

Away from work, I value time with my wife and two children, and I have a hobby of restoring vintage game consoles, right down to board-level repairs. I’m naturally detail-oriented and have the ability to stay focused on a problem until I’ve solved it — traits that serve me well both in my professional projects and personal passions.
