Drone Assist enables safe and responsible flying for both professional and recreational pilots. The app allows users to plan flights, check live airspace data, and request access to controlled zones, making it easier to stay compliant while flying safely.  

I am the sole iOS developer working full time on Drone Assist in a permanent role. I am responsible for the entire iOS implementation, from initial architecture and feature development through to long-term support and maintenance. This involves building almost all non-native UI components from scratch in SwiftUI, integrating backend services, and maintaining Azure DevOps pipelines for continuous delivery. I work closely with an Android developer and a UX designer, translating requirements into intuitive user flows and ensuring feature parity across platforms.  

The app integrates real-time mapping data from multiple APIs and delivers push notifications to alert users about changes in airspace conditions or flight permissions. Accessibility is a key focus area, with full support for Dynamic Type and large text, ensuring the app remains usable in all environments. I also maintain a suite of automated UI tests, as well as analytics and monitoring via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, to guarantee stability in production.  

Drone Assist has an active user base in the UK, typically ranging from 3,000 to 5,000 daily users depending on season and weather. Beyond the UK release, I have designed and maintain a white-labelling system with a feature-flag matrix that allows different regional partners to customise functionality and branding for their local requirements. This system has enabled successful deployments in the Netherlands, Norway, and other regions, with further launches in development.  

My current responsibilities also include ensuring a stable release cycle. Hotfixes are rarely needed, and I focus on incremental updates, performance improvements, and addressing edge cases proactively to maintain long-term reliability.
